## Learning About LASIK Eye Surgery

**LASIK eye surgery** is a type of laser eye surgery that aims at correcting refractive errors such as myopia, hyperopia, or astigmatism. It involves modifying the cornea in such a way that vision is enhanced by improving the focus of light on the retina.

Modern **LASIK laser eye surgery** is a non-invasive surgical technique that can be performed within a short time.

---

## The Importance of the Consultation for LASIK Eye Surgery

Each eye differs from the other, and therefore, a **LASIK eye surgery consultation** becomes crucial before any intervention.

During the consultation, the ophthalmologist conducts a comprehensive eye examination to assess the following:

- Corneal thickness
- Prescription stability and eye power
- General eye health condition
- Quality of the tear film
- Pupil size
- Need for LASIK or another form of vision correction surgery

## Other Methods of Vision Correction

Not everyone is a suitable candidate for LASIK. Depending on your eye condition, your ophthalmologist may recommend other procedures, such as:

- **PRK** for patients with thin corneas
- **ICL** for very high refractive errors
- **RLE** for certain age-related vision problems
- **CLE** in specific clinical situations

The most suitable treatment depends on each patient's individual eye condition.
